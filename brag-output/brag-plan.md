# Brag Plan: InvestWise

## What is this app?
InvestWise is an enterprise investment management platform: members, deposits, funds, projects, dividends, meetings, governance, and reports in one multi-tenant ERP. Every money movement is ledgered, tenant-scoped, and reconciled.

## The angle
InvestWise is the ERP for a real investment club/cooperative running real money. The video sells the calm of control: members → deposits → funds → dividends → reconciliation, all in one polished workspace. Not generic SaaS — show the actual screens and the actual money flow.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On the dark ink background, one line types in: "Every deposit. Every share. Accounted for."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The dashboard materializes: KPI stat cards (total assets, members, dividends distributed) arrive one by one over the real dashboard layout.
- Deposit desk: member selected, fund selected, amount keyed, transaction posts with a reference number.
- Dividend run: scope picked, shares-weighted split preview, "distributed" — receipts per member.

## Outro / punchline
Logo + "InvestWise" + tagline "Enterprise Investment Management Platform" (verbatim from app metadata). Quiet fade.

## User flow worth showing
Member → Deposit → Fund balance → Dividend run → Reconcile. Entry = dashboard/members, key action = post a deposit, result = balances and dividends update. The dashboard, deposit module, and dividend module are the centerpiece scenes.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium enterprise product film
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds, restrained motion, soft crossfades. Confidence through restraint.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Dark ink background. The line "Every deposit. Every share. Accounted for." types in. Teal cursor. A thin teal rule draws under it.
Sequential/interaction: yes — per-character typing
Audio intent: quiet anticipation, key ticks
Audio-coupled idea: keyboard keypress ticks, randomized
Music: fade-in bed
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Dashboard reveal — 6s
Recreate the real dashboard: sidebar shell on the left, header, and KPI stat cards arriving one by one over a hairline-bordered grid; a compact members/transactions table sits below. Teal numbers on dark cards.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 stat cards arrive one by one, table rows cascade
Audio intent: satisfying build, each card lands
Audio-coupled idea: card-place/drop SFX per card arrival
Music: continues, cards snap to beat grid
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Post a deposit — 6s
Recreate the deposit desk: Member select, Fund select, Month select, amount "12,500.00" keyed, method pills, Save button. Click → toast "Deposit saved" → transaction row appears in the ledger with a reference number.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated cursor selects and clicks; amount counts up; toast slides in
Audio intent: decisive, confirming
Audio-coupled idea: click on selects/save, drop on toast
Music: continues
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Dividend run + outro — 5s
Beat A (2.5s): dividend scope "Global", preview lines "Member A +2,400.00 … Member N", Confirm → "Distributed" status pill. Beat B (2.5s): fade to logo lockup — "InvestWise" wordmark + "Enterprise Investment Management Platform".
Sequential/interaction: yes — preview split lines arrive row by row; confirm click
Audio intent: payoff, then calm settle
Audio-coupled idea: chips-stack on preview rows, impactBell on "Distributed", soft land on logo
Music: fade under logo
Transition mood: hold → end

**Music mood for this video:** polished / steady corporate bed at low-mid volume.
**Audio summary:** A quiet premium bed that rises as the dashboard builds, ticks with the deposit flow, rings once on the dividend payoff, and fades under the logo.
